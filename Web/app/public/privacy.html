Privacy policy

Effective 24 September 2026

This policy covers the A3EM Dashboard at config.a3em.com and the optional A3EM Card Helper: a program for your computer, and the browser extension the dashboard reaches it through. They are made by the A3EM project, whose source code is public at github.com/vu-a3em/a3em-dashboard.

In short
  • The dashboard runs in your browser. What is on your SD cards — recordings, logs, GPS tracks — is read there and never sent anywhere.
  • You can use everything without an account. If you create one, it holds your email address, your name, and the protocols you save, kept by Google's Firebase service so you can use them on any computer.
  • There are no advertisements, no analytics, and no tracking. Nothing is sold or shared for marketing.
  • The A3EM Card Helper, program and extension alike, sends nothing over the internet.

What stays on your computer

Your SD cards. When you open a card, the dashboard reads its files in your browser to show you its settings, recordings, logs, device information and any GPS tracks. None of this is uploaded. When you copy a card, the files go to a folder on your computer that you choose. Configuration files you write go onto the card itself. Because a card may hold the locations of animals being studied, this matters: those locations stay with the card and with you.

What the dashboard remembers in your browser, so your work is still there next time:

  • the deployment you are configuring, including any site coordinates and dates you enter;
  • the protocols you save, when you are not signed in;
  • which sections of the page you have opened or closed;
  • permission to reopen the card folder you last chose.

These are kept in your browser's own storage for this site. They never leave your computer, and clearing this site's data in your browser removes them. The dashboard sets no cookies of its own.

If you create an account

Accounts are optional. Their only purpose is to keep your saved protocols, so they are there on any computer you sign in on. They are provided through Firebase, a Google service: Firebase Authentication handles signing in, and Cloud Firestore stores your protocols.

What an account holds

Your email address and nameFrom the service you sign in with, or as you enter them.
How you sign in Which sign-in services you use (you can add more than one to the same account), their identifiers for your account, and when you created the account and last signed in. If you sign in with an email address and password, Firebase stores the password only in a scrambled (hashed) form; the A3EM project never sees it.
Your saved protocolsTheir names, descriptions, versions, dates and recording settings.

An account holds nothing about your cards, recordings, GPS tracks or deployments, other than what you choose to put in a protocol's name or description.

Signing in with Google, GitHub, Apple or Microsoft

When you sign in with one of these services, it tells us your email address and name, and nothing else from your account with it. Signing in happens in a window on the project's Firebase sign-in page. Your use of that service is covered by its own privacy policy. If you sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email address, Apple gives us a private relay address instead of your real one.

What Google processes

Google runs Firebase on the A3EM project's behalf, as a data processor under Google's Firebase Data Processing and Security Terms. To sign you in and protect accounts from abuse, Firebase also processes your IP address and your browser's user agent; Google keeps logged IP addresses for a few weeks. See Privacy and Security in Firebase. Account data is stored in Google's data centers in the United States.

Who can see it

Only you can read or change your protocols through the dashboard: the database refuses anyone else. The people who run the A3EM project can see the database through Firebase's administration console, and use that access only to keep the service working. We do not sell, rent or share account information with anyone.

On your computer while you are signed in

Firebase keeps you signed in, and keeps a copy of your protocols in your browser so they work without an internet connection. Signing out removes that copy from the computer; your protocols stay in your account.

Deleting your account

Choose your account button at the top right of the dashboard, then Account settings, then Delete account. This deletes your saved protocols and your account at once. Google then removes them from its backup systems within 180 days. Accounts are not deleted for inactivity. You can also ask us to delete an account, using the contact details below.

The website itself

The dashboard's pages are served by GitHub Pages, and the A3EM Card Helper is downloaded from GitHub. Like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address when you visit or download, and handles it under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. The A3EM project does not receive these logs.

The A3EM Card Helper

The A3EM Card Helper's browser extension passes messages between the dashboard and its program on the same computer. It collects nothing, keeps nothing, and makes no network requests of its own.

Its program has no network code at all: it works only with the browser that starts it and the cards and disks on your computer. It keeps two things in your user settings folder (~/Library/Application Support/A3EM/card-helper on macOS, %APPDATA%\A3EM\card-helper on Windows, ~/.config/A3EM/card-helper on Linux):

  • a key it uses to sign the confirmations you give before it erases a card, and a record of which it has used;
  • a record of the cards it has prepared — their size, format, label and test results — so that a later check can report them.

If you ask it to copy a whole card to a file, the copy is saved on your computer, in the A3EM card images folder in your Documents. Uninstalling the program leaves the settings folder in place; delete it to remove those records.

Children

The dashboard is a tool for researchers and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly create accounts for children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If what the dashboard or the A3EM Card Helper does with data changes, this page will change first, with a new effective date at the top. Past versions are in the project's public history.

Contact

Questions, or a request to see or delete your data: open an issue at github.com/vu-a3em/a3em-dashboard/issues. Please do not include personal details in a public issue; say that you have a privacy request, and we will arrange to continue privately.
